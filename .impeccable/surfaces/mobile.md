## Direction contract

THESIS: A legible daily agenda makes the next step obvious. Peggy sees a short personal day; family sees the coordination behind it. Replace floating top tabs and the dark wall of actions.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral paper ground, white surfaces, charcoal ink, hairline separators, 16pt rounded task surfaces and amber lamp. Atkinson Hyperlegible for Peggy; system type for family. Family density is deliberately higher, with compact rows and dated appointment blocks.

STORY: Read today's status, see the next plan, check in or act. Find everyone and every plan from persistent labeled tabs. Status changes are described in words.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Senior: slim brand bar; large Today, Peggy title; date and daily-call row; next appointment; full-width 104pt check-in button; persistent Today / My plans / Family tabs. Family: Today title; Peggy status and summary; attention rows; next plan; Today / Plans / Family / Settings tabs. Content scrolls under neither header nor tabs.

FORM: The daily page, grounded candidate 1; seed `882a2259` assigned candidate 5, user selected pick. Seven candidates: daily agenda, civic wayfinding, garden labels, timetable, personal correspondence, family recipe cards, patchwork rhythm. All respect native navigation. Signature: the same dated next-plan row connects both experiences, with Peggy's readable next action and family's transport state. Existing lamp lights after successful check-in; no tab animation.
